# Air Compressor Maintenance — Atlas Copco GA11 Series

## Cooling System
- Air-cooled oil cooler must be cleaned monthly. Use compressed air (max 3 bar) to blow out fins.
- Oil temperature should not exceed 90°C at discharge. High temp triggers thermal shutdown at 105°C.
- Check oil cooler differential temperature. >15°C above ambient indicates restricted airflow.

## Air Filter
- Replace air filter element every 2000 hours or when differential pressure reaches 25 mbar.
- In dusty environments, reduce interval to 1000 hours.
- Never operate without air filter — causes accelerated wear of compression element.

## Oil Separator
- Replace oil separator when differential pressure exceeds 0.8 bar.
- Typical life: 4000–8000 hours depending on oil condition and environment.
- Excessive oil carryover (>3 ppm) indicates saturated separator or wrong oil type.

## Valve Kit
- Inlet and minimum pressure valves should be serviced at 8000-hour intervals.
- Symptoms of valve failure: reduced capacity, high discharge temperature, oil in air receiver.
