# Conveyor Gearbox Maintenance — SEW-Eurodrive K Series

## Oil Change Schedule
- First change at 500 hours, then every 10,000 hours or annually.
- Use SAE 80W-90 gear oil (synthetic preferred for high-load applications).
- Oil capacity: 3.5L (K67 frame). Fill to center of sight glass.

## Belt and Coupling Inspection
- Check belt tension monthly. Deflection should be 10–15mm per 1m span under 10N force.
- Replace belt if cracks visible on inner surface or if tension cannot be maintained.
- Coupling insert (elastomer spider) should show no visible damage or permanent compression.

## Bearing Replacement
- Gearbox bearings (32210 taper roller) typically last 20,000–30,000 hours.
- Symptoms of wear: increasing vibration at shaft rotation frequency, temperature rise, audible rumble.
- Always replace in pairs. Pre-load to manufacturer specification (0.02–0.05mm end play).

## RPM Instability Diagnosis
- Oscillating output speed indicates: worn coupling, bearing backlash, or VFD tuning issue.
- Measure RPM at motor and output shaft simultaneously to isolate mechanical vs electrical cause.
- Check VFD parameters: ramp time, PID gains, motor nameplate data match.
