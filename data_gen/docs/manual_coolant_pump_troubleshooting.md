# Coolant Pump Troubleshooting Guide — Grundfos CRN Series

## Common Failure Modes

### Lubrication Loss
- **Symptoms**: Rising temperature and vibration simultaneously, especially under load.
- **Root Cause**: Shaft seal wear allowing coolant to wash out bearing grease, or grease degradation from contamination.
- **Resolution**: Replace shaft seal (mechanical seal 30mm), repack bearings with Mobil SHC grease, flush housing.

### Impeller Cavitation
- **Symptoms**: Fluctuating discharge pressure, rattling noise, reduced flow rate.
- **Root Cause**: Air entrainment from low coolant level, restricted suction line, or worn impeller.
- **Resolution**: Check coolant level, inspect suction filter, replace impeller if eroded.

## Maintenance Schedule
| Interval | Task |
|----------|------|
| Daily | Check discharge pressure and flow rate |
| Weekly | Inspect for leaks at shaft seal |
| Monthly | Check vibration levels; grease motor bearings |
| Quarterly | Coolant quality test (pH, concentration, bacteria) |
| Annually | Full pump teardown and inspection |
