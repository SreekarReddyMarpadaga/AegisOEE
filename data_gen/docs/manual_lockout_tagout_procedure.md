# Lock-Out/Tag-Out (LOTO) Procedure — All CNC and Rotating Equipment

## Scope
This procedure applies to all maintenance activities on CNC spindles, servo motors, coolant pumps, conveyors, and air compressors at HYD_PRECISION plant.

## Steps
1. Notify production supervisor and affected operators.
2. Shut down equipment using normal stop procedure.
3. Isolate all energy sources:
   - Electrical: Open disconnect switch, apply lock and tag.
   - Pneumatic: Close supply valve, bleed to 0 bar, apply lock.
   - Hydraulic: De-pressurize, block cylinders, apply lock.
4. Verify zero energy state:
   - Attempt to start equipment (must not respond).
   - Verify 0V at motor terminals with multimeter.
   - Verify 0 bar on pressure gauges.
5. Perform maintenance work.
6. Remove tools and replace guards.
7. Remove locks in reverse order. Last lock removed by person who applied it.
8. Notify all personnel before re-energizing.

## Emergency
In event of emergency requiring immediate equipment stop, use E-STOP button. Do not use LOTO for emergency stops.
