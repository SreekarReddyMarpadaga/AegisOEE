# Thermal Management Best Practices — CNC Machining Centers

## Coolant System Overview
- Primary coolant: water-soluble cutting fluid at 5–8% concentration.
- Coolant temperature target: 20–25°C. Variations >5°C affect dimensional accuracy.
- Flow rate: minimum 15 L/min at spindle, 10 L/min at chip conveyor.

## Cooling Restriction Diagnosis
1. Check coolant level in reservoir (minimum: 75% capacity).
2. Inspect suction strainer for chip blockage.
3. Check inline filter differential pressure. Replace >0.5 bar.
4. Verify coolant pump discharge pressure (spec: 3–5 bar).
5. Inspect nozzle alignment and flow at each delivery point.
6. Thermal imaging of spindle: temperature gradient >20°C from base to nose is abnormal.

## Temperature Alarms
| Sensor Location | Warning (°C) | Shutdown (°C) |
|----------------|-------------|--------------|
| Spindle bearing | 65 | 85 |
| Servo motor | 70 | 80 |
| Coolant discharge | 30 | 40 |
| Hydraulic unit | 55 | 65 |
