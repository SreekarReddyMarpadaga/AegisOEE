# Vibration Sensor Calibration — IFM VTV122 Series

## Calibration Procedure
1. Mount sensor on calibration exciter (reference: 10 mm/s RMS at 159.15 Hz).
2. Power sensor with 24VDC supply. Verify 4–20 mA output.
3. At reference excitation, output should read 12.0 ±0.2 mA.
4. If out of spec, adjust zero and span via teach-in button.
5. Record calibration date and deviation in maintenance system.

## Mounting Requirements
- Use M8 stud mount for permanent installation. Torque: 5 Nm.
- Flat mounting surface (Ra <6.3 μm). Machine or hand-lap if necessary.
- Cable routing: avoid proximity to power cables (min 200mm separation).
- Junction box must be IP65 or better. Moisture ingress causes intermittent readings.

## Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Likely Cause | Action |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Flatline at 4 mA | Broken cable or sensor failure | Check cable continuity, replace sensor |
| Erratic spikes | Loose mounting or EMI | Retorque, check cable shield |
| Gradual drift | Sensor aging or contamination | Recalibrate or replace |
| No output | Power supply failure | Check 24V at sensor terminals |
