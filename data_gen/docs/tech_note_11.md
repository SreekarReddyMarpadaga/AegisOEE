# Technician Note — CNC_02_SPINDLE Bearing Replacement
**Asset**: CNC_02_SPINDLE | **Shift**: B | **Tech**: Suresh Patel

Called in for high vibration alarm at 5.8 mm/s. Confirmed bearing wear — kurtosis had been trending
up for 4 days (from 3.2 to 6.1). Inner race pitting visible upon disassembly.
Replaced both bearings with SKF 7014C kit. Applied Kluber grease per spec.
Post-repair vibration: 1.6 mm/s. Machine released back to production after 30-minute test run.
Total repair time: 4.5 hours including LOTO and cleanup.
