# Technician Note — CONVEYOR_GBX_01 Belt Replacement
**Asset**: CONVEYOR_GBX_01 | **Shift**: A | **Tech**: Mohan Das

RPM oscillating ±50 from setpoint. Found drive belt cracked on inner surface with uneven wear.
Belt tensioner spring also weak — couldn't maintain proper tension.
Replaced both belt (HTD-8M-1200) and tensioner assembly. Aligned pulleys with laser tool.
RPM now stable at 1500 ±5. Cycle time variance dropped from 12% to 2%.
