# Technician Note — COOLANT_PUMP_01 Lubrication Issue
**Asset**: COOLANT_PUMP_01 | **Shift**: A | **Tech**: Anand Reddy

Pump running hot (68°C at bearing housing, normally 45°C). Vibration elevated to 4.2 mm/s.
Investigation found shaft seal weeping — coolant contaminating bearing grease.
Replaced mechanical seal and re-greased both bearings. Flushed housing with solvent before repacking.
Temperature dropped to 46°C within 20 min of restart. Will monitor daily for a week.
